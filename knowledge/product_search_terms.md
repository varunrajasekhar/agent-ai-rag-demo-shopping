# Retailer Search Vocabulary by Product Type

These terms translate broad shopping intentions into phrases that retailers
are more likely to use. They are alternative searches, not words that must all
appear on one product.

## Shirts and blouses

For a professional shirt, useful retailer terms include button-up, collared,
cotton poplin, tailored, and wrinkle resistant. For comfort, alternatives
include linen blend, linen, cotton, viscose, rayon, breathable, lightweight,
relaxed fit, and regular fit.

## Tops

For professional or polished tops, useful retailer terms include collared,
cotton poplin, and regular fit. For comfort, alternatives include linen blend,
linen, cotton, viscose, rayon, breathable, lightweight, and relaxed fit.

## Pants and trousers

For professional pants, useful retailer terms include tailored, straight fit,
and wide-leg. For comfort, alternatives include relaxed fit, linen blend,
linen, cotton, viscose, breathable, lightweight, and stretch.

## Blazers and jackets

For professional blazers, useful retailer terms include tailored and
unstructured. For comfort, alternatives include linen blend, linen, cotton,
lightweight, breathable, stretch, and wrinkle resistant.

## Dresses

For professional dresses, useful retailer terms include shirt dress and midi
dress. For comfort, alternatives include linen blend, linen, cotton, viscose,
rayon, breathable, lightweight, and relaxed fit.

## Skirts

For professional skirts, useful retailer terms include midi skirt and tailored.
For comfort, alternatives include linen blend, linen, cotton, viscose,
lightweight, and stretch.


## Shoes

For professional and comfortable footwear, useful retailer terms include
loafers, flat shoes, dress shoes, low heels, sneakers, and leather shoes.

Loafers are a versatile option for professional outfits.
Flat or low-profile shoes can provide comfort for extended wear.
Dress shoes can work well for more formal professional outfits.
Clean, minimal sneakers may work for business-casual outfits.
